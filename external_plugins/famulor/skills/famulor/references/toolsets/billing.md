# Billing toolset

Balance, usage, transactions, invoices, billing recovery, and referrals. Connect only this group with `https://app.famulor.io/mcp?toolsets=billing`.

This 2026-10-08 snapshot covers all 22 tools assigned to `billing` in the canonical 439-tool registry. The live MCP `tools/list` response is authoritative for arguments, current availability, annotations, and plan or role gating. Never invent fields from this catalog.

Toolsets limit discovery context; they do not grant access. OAuth/API scopes and workspace roles are enforced separately. The Effect column reflects MCP risk annotations: `Delete/destructive` also covers overwrites or changes that can remove data or safeguards.

**Caution:** Billing and reseller-plan changes can affect invoices, taxes, credits, or customer entitlements. Confirm the workspace or customer plan and the financial effect before changing them.

| `cancel_addon_trial` | Delete/destructive | `billing:write` | Immediate | Cancel automatic renewal of an add-on trial subscription. Access remains until its paid or trial period ends. Requires a user-owned billing:write credential and owner, admin, or billing access. |
| `confirm_subscription_payment_change` | Write/action | `billing:write` | Immediate | Apply a previously reviewed subscription change. May charge the selected saved card and change its future renewal method. Set confirm=true only after explicit user approval of the reviewed selection, amount, currency and card. Use references from the same review; omit the card only when no payment is due. A pending payment does not grant the requested upgrade; open the returned billing link to finish payment. Requires a user-owned billing:write credential and owner, admin, or billing access. |
| `create_billing_portal_link` | Write/action | `billing:write` | Immediate | Create a short-lived first-party link for viewing invoices, updating payment methods, and managing cancellation. | `create_invoice_payment_link` | Write/action | `billing:write` | Immediate | Create a short-lived first-party link for paying the workspace's outstanding subscription invoice. Requires billing:write and an owner, admin, or billing role. The link contains no payment-provider or internal resource identifiers. |
| `create_reseller_tax_registration` | Delete/destructive | `billing:write` | Immediate | Add or schedule tax collection for a jurisdiction where the business is registered. This does not register the business with a tax authority. Obtain explicit owner instructions for jurisdiction and effective dates. Registrations cannot be deleted. Reuse request_key only when retrying the same creation request. Requires billing:write and owner or admin access. |

| `get_referrals` | Read-only | `billing:read or calls:read` | Immediate | Get the workspace Refer and Earn share link, reward amounts, and referral history. Only available on the main platform. Friend emails are masked. Required scope: billing:read or calls:read. |
