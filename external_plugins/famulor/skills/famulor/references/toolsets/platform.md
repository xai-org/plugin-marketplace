# Platform toolset

Authorized reseller customer administration. Connect only this group with `https://app.famulor.io/mcp?toolsets=platform`.

This 2026-10-08 snapshot covers all 16 tools assigned to `platform` in the canonical 439-tool registry. The live MCP `tools/list` response is authoritative for arguments, current availability, annotations, and plan or role gating. Never invent fields from this catalog.

Toolsets limit discovery context; they do not grant access. OAuth/API scopes and workspace roles are enforced separately. The Effect column reflects MCP risk annotations: `Delete/destructive` also covers overwrites or changes that can remove data or safeguards.

**Caution:** Reseller administration can affect customer accounts and credits. Verify the customer workspace, authority, and intended change before execution.

| Tool | Effect | Accepted scope | Execution | Purpose snapshot |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `create_custom_payment` | Write/action | `platform:write` | Immediate | Charge a customer a one-off amount or start a custom recurring subscription — outside their regular plan. Delivery channel decides how the customer pays: 'charge_saved_method' bills their card on file immediately (fails if none is on file or it needs authentication), 'invoice' sends a hosted invoice due in days_until_due days, 'payment_link' creates a single-use payment link (never expires, works without a saved card). Automatic tax calculation applies when available for the billing account and the customer has a billing address on file. Moves real money — confirm the amount, currency and delivery channel with the operator before calling. |
| `create_platform_user_token` | Write/action | `platform:write` | Immediate | Mint an API key for one of your platform's customers, identified by user_id or email. This is the CUSTOMER's own platform credential (created_by = the customer, not you) — it stops working automatically if the customer ever leaves their workspace. The plaintext key is returned EXACTLY ONCE and is never shown or stored again; hand it to the customer or store it immediately. |
| `get_custom_payment` | Read-only | `platform:read` | Immediate | Fetch a single custom payment by ID: amounts, tax, status, hosted link/invoice PDF when available, and which actions currently apply to it. |
| `get_platform_default_limits` | Read-only | `platform:read` | Immediate | Get whether customers can register themselves, whether customers without a paid plan may use prepaid credits, the prepaid extra-minute price in EUR, and the limits applied to prepaid accounts. |
| `get_platform_user` | Read-only | `platform:read` | Immediate | Fetch a single platform customer's detail: profile, every workspace they have in your scope, and balances. |
| `get_platform_user_password_access` | Read-only | `platform:read` | Immediate | Check whether you can set a customer's password or send them a branded reset email. Direct changes require an account used exclusively within your platform. |
| `get_platform_welcome_credit_settings` | Read-only | `platform:read` | Immediate | Get the one-time wallet credits automatically offered to each new free customer, whether automatic grants are active, your current wallet balance, and how many customers that balance can currently fund. |
| `list_custom_payments` | Read-only | `platform:read` | Immediate | List admin-created one-time charges, invoices and subscriptions for your platform's customers: a reseller's own customer workspaces, or — for a platform admin — every root workspace. Filter by user_id to see one customer's payments. Supports status/kind/channel filters, a search (q) that matches the payment description AND the customer's e-mail, and cursor pagination. |
| `list_platform_users` | Read-only | `platform:read` | Immediate | List your platform's end-user customers: a reseller's own child workspaces, or — for a platform admin — every platform root workspace (never another reseller's customers). Supports limit/offset pagination and a name/email search (q). |
| `logout_platform_user` | Delete/destructive | `platform:write` | Immediate | Revoke every active API key and OAuth token belonging to one of your platform's customers, across all of their workspaces in your scope (never a workspace under a different reseller). Idempotent — logging out an already-logged-out customer returns zero counts. |
| `manage_platform_user_password` | Delete/destructive | `platform:write` | Immediate | Send a branded password reset email (action reset), or set a new password for a customer account used exclusively within your platform (action set). Never changes another platform's customers or administrator accounts. The password is not returned. |
| `register_platform_user` | Write/action | `platform:write` | Immediate | Create a new customer account on your platform. mode 'invite' (default) emails an invite with no password required; mode 'password' requires an 8+ character password. Fails with a generic 'account already exists' error whether the email is already used inside or outside your scope (anti-enumeration). |
| `run_custom_payment_action` | Delete/destructive | `platform:write` | Immediate | Act on an existing custom payment. refresh re-syncs status/amounts from the billing provider. resend_notification re-sends the customer notification e-mail. cancel stops it — for a one-time invoice it voids the invoice, for a payment link it deactivates it; for a subscription it cancels at the end of the current period unless immediately is true, in which case it cancels right now. cancel_at_period_end schedules a subscription to cancel at the end of its current period (subscriptions only; same effect as cancel without immediately). mark_uncollectible writes off an open invoice as uncollectible. deactivate_link disables a payment link without canceling anything already paid. Canceling or writing off is not reversible through this API — confirm with the operator first. |
| `transfer_platform_credits` | Write/action | `platform:write` | Immediate | Move in-app service credits (usage units — never money) between your own workspace and a customer's workspace, identified by user_id or email. Positive credits grants from you to the customer; negative credits reclaims back from the customer. A reclaim fails with an error if the customer's wallet does not hold enough to cover it — balances never go below zero. |
| `update_platform_default_limits` | Write/action | `platform:write` | Immediate | Update self-service registration, prepaid access, the prepaid extra-minute price in EUR, or prepaid account limits. Registration and prepaid access are independent controls. |
| `update_platform_welcome_credit_settings` | Write/action | `platform:write` | Immediate | Set the one-time wallet credits offered to future free customers. Use 0 to disable automatic grants. Saving is allowed even when the amount exceeds your current wallet balance. |
