# OsmO AI Receptionist

The inbound companion of [OsmO Phone Calls](../osmo): an AI phone receptionist and answering
service for your own number, run from Grok.

- MCP server: `https://api.getosmo.app/mcp/receptionist` (Streamable HTTP, OAuth 2.1 with PKCE;
  no API key). Sign in once with your phone number.
- Needs the OsmO app (https://getosmo.app/app) with a plan and call forwarding on.
- Every call back and every settings change is shown first and happens only after you say yes.
