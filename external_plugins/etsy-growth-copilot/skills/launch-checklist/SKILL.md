# Etsy listing launch checklist

Work through every item. Do not publish, change a price, or turn on ads. Send the finished checklist to the owner and wait for explicit approval.

1. **Photos**: at least 5 real photos: hero, plain background, scale, detail or fit, and in use. Alt text on each.
2. **SEO**: title, 13 tags, description, and attributes are done. Run the listing-seo-pack skill if they are not. Title max 140 characters with the main buyer phrase in the first ~40. All 13 tags, each max 20 characters. No pipes, no keyword stuffing, and no implied brand affiliation ("fits X" only).
3. **Variations**: all options set, prices per variation correct, and a sensible default selected.
4. **Price and shipping**: margin checked after fees and shipping. Fee reference (re-verify on Etsy before quoting): $0.20 listing fee renewed every 4 months, 6.5% transaction fee, US processing 3% + $0.25. Items under $10 can see fixed fees around 15%. US search favors the free-shipping guarantee on orders of $35 or more; listings that charge $6 or more for shipping have been demoted since October 2024. Bundle or pair to clear $35 when that is viable. Keep shipping under $6 or free.
5. **Quantity**: realistic stock.
6. **Processing time**: honest, and it matches production capacity.
7. **Section**: assigned to a section. Section names are max 24 characters. Shop headline, if you are drafting one, is max 55 characters.
8. **Cross-links**: the description links to related listings, and those listings link back where that is relevant.
9. **Claims and compliance**: no unverifiable claims. Include a license credit line if the design requires one. Never fake reviews or favorites or offer incentives for them. A thank-you card asking for a photo review is fine.
10. **Owner review**: send the full listing to the owner and wait for explicit approval.
11. **Publish and log**: publish only after that approval, then log the date, listing URL, price, and main keyword in the tracker.

Label any numbers `[FACT]`, `[EST]`, or `[NOTES]`. If Etsy blocks automated access, stop and ask for screenshots.
