# Listing SEO pack

Do not publish the listing. Deliver copy for the owner to approve.

## Inputs

- Product (what it is, material, size, variations, what is included)
- Target buyer and use (who buys it, and for what room or occasion)
- Seed keywords (from the owner, Etsy search autocomplete, or Stats search terms)
- 3–5 competitor listings ranking for the main phrase (titles, tags if visible, price, photos)

## Steps

1. Pick the main buyer phrase: what a shopper actually types. Check Etsy autocomplete and competitors. Note 2–3 secondary phrases.
2. Title (max 140 characters): main phrase in the first ~40 characters, then secondary phrases in natural language. No pipes, no keyword stuffing, no repeated words. For compatible products say "fits X". Never imply brand affiliation.
3. Tags: all 13, each max 20 characters, multi-word. Do not just repeat the title. Mix exact product terms, compatibility long-tails, use or room, and gift or occasion.
4. Description in this order: benefit first line; what is included; fit and size; variations; care; shipping; compliance notes; credit line (if a licensed design needs attribution); cross-sell to related listings.
5. Attributes: fill every one available (color, material, occasion, holiday, room, and the rest). They feed search filters.
6. Alt text for each photo: a plain description of what the photo shows, including the product name once.
7. Claims check: remove anything unverifiable (for example "FDA food safe"). Use careful wording.

## Output

- Title, 13 tags (with character counts), full description, attribute list, and alt text per photo.
- Checklist: title length OK, main phrase in the first 40 characters, 13 tags all ≤20 characters, attributes filled, 5 or more real photos (hero, plain background, scale, detail or fit, in use), no unverifiable claims, section assigned.
- Label any numbers `[FACT]`, `[EST]`, or `[NOTES]`. Nothing is published without the owner's approval.
