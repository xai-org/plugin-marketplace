# Etsy weekly sales tracker

If Etsy blocks automated access, stop and ask for screenshots. Do not publish, reprice, or change ads from this skill. Flag actions for the owner to approve.

1. **Collect** (the owner's signed-in Etsy Stats, or screenshots) for the last 7 days: visits, views, orders, revenue, conversion rate, favorites, traffic sources, and top search terms. Per listing, also collect ad spend, clicks, CPC, orders, and ROAS.
2. **Record** one row per week in the owner's spreadsheet (Google Sheets or OneDrive Excel, whichever they chose). Create the sheet with those columns if it does not exist.
3. **Week-over-week**: compute the change for each metric and call out the biggest 3 moves, up or down.
4. **SEO refresh triggers** (flag the listing for a listing-seo-pack rewrite):
   - Views down 30% or more for 2 weeks in a row
   - Views but conversion under 1% over 30 days
   - 0 views in 90 days (renew with new SEO, or deactivate)
   - A new top search term the listing does not target
5. **Report**: a short summary, the 3 biggest moves, flagged listings, and one recommended action. Label numbers `[FACT]`, `[EST]`, or `[NOTES]`.
