# Etsy store audit

Work from the owner's signed-in Etsy session or their screenshots. Etsy blocks bots. If a page is blocked, stop and ask for screenshots. Do not change the shop. Draft fixes and wait for the owner's approval.

Do not invent a shop story, a person's name, an email, or a private link. Use only facts the owner confirms.

1. **Featured 4**: are the top 4 featured listings the best sellers or best-converting items, with strong hero photos?
2. **Sections**: logical groups, names max 24 characters, every listing in a section, and no empty sections.
3. **Shop headline**: max 55 characters, and it says what the shop sells in buyer words.
4. **Announcement**: keep an evergreen version (what you make, shipping promise) and a promo version for sales. Swap them as needed.
5. **About**: a story built only from owner-confirmed facts (who makes it, how, and where). Never invent details.
6. **Banner**: 3360×840 px, with key content inside the 1200×300 center safe zone.
7. **Shipping**: free-shipping guarantee on $35+ orders if that is viable. US search favors that guarantee. Listings that charge $6 or more for shipping have been demoted since October 2024, so flag every listing in that range.
8. **Stale listings**: find listings with 0 views in 90 days. Recommend renew-with-new-SEO or deactivate for each one.
9. **Policies and branding**: policies filled, and a consistent logo and photo style.
10. **Reviews**: never recommend faking reviews or favorites, and never recommend incentives for them. A thank-you card asking for a photo review is fine.

## Output

Findings per item, a prioritized fix list with a recommended first fix, and drafts for any text changes. No changes go live without the owner's approval. Label numbers `[FACT]`, `[EST]`, or `[NOTES]`.
