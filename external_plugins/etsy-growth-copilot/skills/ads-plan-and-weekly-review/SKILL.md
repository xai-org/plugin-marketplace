# Etsy Ads plan and weekly review

Recommend only. Do not change the daily budget, turn ads on or off, or spend money without the owner's explicit approval. If Etsy blocks automated access, stop and ask for screenshots.

## Mechanics (re-verify on Etsy)

One daily budget, and Etsy auto-bids. Per-listing strategies unlock at $25/day. Minimum is $1/day. Attribution is an ad click and then an order within 30 days.

Offsite Ads: 15% of attributed orders, capped at $100 per order. Opt-out is allowed under $10k of sales in 365 days. Above that, the fee is 12% and mandatory.

## Plan

1. **Margins**: for each candidate listing, price, total cost, fees, and margin %. Fee reference (re-verify on Etsy before quoting): $0.20 listing fee renewed every 4 months, 6.5% transaction fee, US processing 3% + $0.25. Items under $10 can see fixed fees around 15%.
2. **Break-even table** per listing: break-even ROAS = 1 / margin; max cost per order = price × margin; break-even conversion = CPC / max cost per order. Target ROAS 2.0 or higher. Benchmarks (third-party, overridden by the seller's own data): 1.5–3× ROAS, $0.10–$0.60 CPC, 1–3% conversion.
3. **Tiers**: Tier A = proven sellers with good margin (advertise). Tier B = promising, test. Tier C = never advertise (custom items, very cheap add-ons, unproven drafts).
4. **Budget steps with triggers**: start small ($1–5/day) on 2–3 listings and rotate. Step up only after ROAS is at or above target for 2 consecutive weeks. Step down if results are below break-even.
5. **Rules**: kill a listing after 0 orders at spend of about 1.5 × max cost per order. Clicks but no orders means fix the listing (photos, price, title), not the ads. Check whether ads only buy sales on listings that already rank #1 organically. Change one thing at a time, at most every 7 days. Judge only after 30 or more days, or 30–50 or more clicks per listing.
6. **4-week test calendar**: week 1 launch, weeks 2–3 hold steady and collect data, week 4 decide kill, keep, or scale per listing.

## Monday review

1. Pull per-listing spend, clicks, CPC, orders, revenue, and ROAS for the last 7 and 30 days (the owner's Stats or screenshots).
2. Compare them to the break-even table. Flag kills, fixes, and scale candidates.
3. Give a decision menu with a recommended option. No budget or ad change without the owner's approval.
4. Log the week in the tracker. Label numbers `[FACT]`, `[EST]`, or `[NOTES]`.
