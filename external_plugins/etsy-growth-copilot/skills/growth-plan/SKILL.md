# Etsy growth plan

Plan only. Do not publish listings, change prices, or spend on ads without the owner's explicit approval. Give decisions as a menu with one recommended option. Label numbers `[FACT]`, `[EST]`, or `[NOTES]`. Use only facts the owner confirms.

1. **Baseline**: current monthly visits, orders, revenue, conversion, active listings, and review count, from Stats. If Etsy blocks automated access, stop and ask for screenshots.
2. **Milestones with gates**, for example:
   - Foundation: every listing passes the launch checklist, and store-audit fixes are done.
   - Traction: steady weekly orders, and conversion at or above 1–3%.
   - Scale: proven listings profitable on ads (ROAS 2.0 or higher), then expand the winning product lines.
   Move to the next stage only when the gate metric holds for 4 weeks.
3. **Product pipeline**: run product validation monthly and launch the top Go items only after the owner approves each one.
4. **Seasonal calendar**: map the next 6–12 months, including Valentine's Day, Mother's Day, Father's Day, back to school, Halloween, and Q4. For Q4, have gift listings and tags live by early October, plan Black Friday and Cyber Monday sales (set them up in Etsy ahead of time), post holiday shipping cutoffs in the announcement and in listings (check current carrier dates each year), and stock up.
5. **30-day review**: what moved, what did not, and which priorities to adjust.
6. **90-day review**: compare to the baseline, re-set milestones, and drop what is not working.
7. **Ads and fees**: when the plan depends on ad profit or margin, use the ads break-even rules (target ROAS 2.0 or higher; break-even ROAS = 1 / margin) and re-verify Etsy fees before quoting them. Fee reference: $0.20 listing fee renewed every 4 months, 6.5% transaction fee, US processing 3% + $0.25.
