# Etsy core rules

Apply these rules on every Etsy task. Each task skill repeats the rules it uses, because a plugin does not remember skills that were not loaded. These are reference facts: re-verify fees, ads rules, and shipping policy on Etsy before quoting them as current. The seller's own data overrides any benchmark.

Keep the work store-agnostic. Do not invent a shop name, a person's name, an email, or a private link. Use only facts the owner confirms.

## Ways of working

- Label every number `[FACT]`, `[EST]`, or `[NOTES]`.
- Give decisions as a menu with one recommended option.
- Never publish a listing, change a price, change an ad, or spend money without the owner's explicit approval.
- If Etsy blocks automated access, stop and ask for screenshots. Do not try to get around the block.

## Listing SEO

- Titles: up to 140 characters. Put the main buyer phrase in the first ~40 characters. No pipes and no keyword stuffing. For compatible products say "fits X". Never imply brand affiliation.
- Tags: use all 13. Each tag is up to 20 characters and should be a multi-word phrase. Do not just repeat the title. Mix exact product terms, compatibility long-tails, use or room, and gift or occasion.
- Description order: benefit first line, what is included, fit and size, variations, care, shipping, compliance notes, credit line, cross-sell.
- Fill every attribute (color, material, occasion, holiday, room).
- At least 5 real photos: hero, plain background, scale, detail or fit, in use.
- No unverifiable claims (for example "FDA food safe"). Use careful wording.

## Shop structure

- Shop headline: up to 55 characters.
- Section names: up to 24 characters. Every listing belongs in a section.

## Reviews

Never fake reviews or favorites, and never offer incentives for them. A thank-you card asking for a photo review is fine.

## Shipping and search

US search favors the free-shipping guarantee on orders of $35 or more. Listings that charge $6 or more for shipping have been demoted since October 2024. Bundle to clear $35 when that is viable.

## Fees

- $0.20 listing fee, renewed every 4 months.
- 6.5% transaction fee.
- US payment processing: 3% + $0.25.
- Items under $10: fixed fees are about 15%.

Re-verify on Etsy before quoting.

## Etsy Ads

- One daily budget. Etsy auto-bids. Per-listing strategies unlock at $25/day. Minimum $1/day. Attribution is an ad click followed by an order within 30 days.
- Judge a listing only after 30 or more days, or 30–50 or more clicks on that listing. Change one thing at a time, at most every 7 days.
- Break-even ROAS = 1 / margin. Max cost per order = price × margin. Break-even conversion = CPC / max cost per order. Target ROAS 2.0 or higher.
- Third-party benchmarks, overridden by the seller's own data: 1.5–3× ROAS, $0.10–$0.60 CPC, 1–3% conversion.
- Small budget: advertise 2–3 listings and rotate. Never advertise custom items, very cheap add-ons, or unproven drafts.
- Kill rule: 0 orders after spend of about 1.5 × max cost per order. Clicks without orders means fix the listing. Check whether ads are only buying sales on a listing that already ranks #1 organically.

## Offsite Ads

15% of the order, capped at $100 per order. Opt-out is allowed under $10k of sales in 365 days. Above that, the fee is 12% and mandatory.

## Product validation and licenses

Score demand, competition, price range, cost and margin, shipping risk, and IP or trademark exposure. For design files: CC0, public domain, CC BY, and MIT allow selling (give credit where the license requires it). CC BY-SA allows selling with credit and share-alike. NC does not allow selling. Save proof with the URL and the date.

## Weekly snapshot

Record visits, views, orders, revenue, conversion, favorites, traffic sources, top search terms, and per-listing ad spend, clicks, CPC, orders, and ROAS.
